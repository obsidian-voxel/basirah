Basirah blurs explicit content before you see it, and blocks the apps, sites
and keywords that lead to it. Everything runs on your phone: the app has no
INTERNET permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere, even by
mistake.

HOW THE BLUR WORKS
An Android accessibility service sees which app is in front. When the screen
changes, Basirah takes one frame, classifies it on the device with models
bundled inside the app, and if nudity or exposed skin is detected it draws a
blurred copy of that frame over the screen. The frame is then discarded.
Nothing is written to storage and nothing is uploaded.

BLOCKING LAYERS
- Apps: close the app when it opens, and hide its launcher icon.
- Sites: block by host or URL pattern in any browser.
- Keywords: flag the search queries and page text you choose.
- Calm mode: allow a short list of apps and keep the session from being
  exited.
- Schedules and daily limits: protection that follows your day.

TWO PROTECTION MODES
- Standard works on any Android 11+ phone with the accessibility grant alone.
- Hardened is optional and applies only when Basirah is your device owner. It
  adds anti-removal policies -- and it reports honestly when a policy is not
  actually in effect. There are no green ticks for anything Android has not
  confirmed.

PRIVACY BY CONSTRUCTION
No account, no cloud, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no
update checks. Block lists, detection history and settings live in the app's
private storage and are deleted when you uninstall.

WHAT BASIRAH DOES NOT DO
It does not restore deleted files, hide itself from Settings, or stop someone
who knows the device PIN from uninstalling it. It is not a parental-control
service and it cannot watch another phone.
